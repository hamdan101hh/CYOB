Visual direction

Moodboard & art direction

Palette

Indigo, warm grey, bronze, rose, paper white. Sparing use of pure black.

Lighting

Natural and low. First light, last light. Restrained interior lamp. Avoid harsh midday or fluorescent.

Reference voices

Roger Deakins, Hou Hsiao-Hsien, Andreas Gursky, Hiroshi Sugimoto, Wim Wenders, Luigi Ghirri.

Composition

Wide and still, or tight and intimate. Avoid mid-range telephoto banality. Negative space is luxury.

Casting

Real customers, not models. Linen, cashmere, neutral wardrobes. No styled luxury cliché.

Sound

Ambient, restrained, often silent. No music swell. The pause is the point.
